Research

My work connects tumor heterogeneity, immune cell states, and the places where they interact.

01 — SPATIAL GENOMICS

Mapping glioblastoma from core to edge

I led the analysis of a 22-sample Visium cohort with matched Xenium and single-nucleus RNA-seq to map tumor and immune states across anatomical niches. Niche-restricted interaction analysis reveals how cellular communication depends on tissue context.

02 — TUMOR IMMUNOLOGY

Understanding checkpoint blockade response

Using single-cell transcriptomes from syngeneic glioma models, I studied divergent responses to PD-1 blockade, linking response to progenitor versus terminally exhausted CD8+ T-cell states and the influence of CD4 T cells.

03 — SINGLE-CELL MULTI-OMICS

Decoding tumor ecosystems and evolution

I integrate genetic and transcriptional data to connect mutations with cellular programs, from meningioma and the vestibular schwannoma ecosystem to relapse-associated states in acute myeloid leukemia.

Learning the signals of RNA modification
