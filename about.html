About & curriculum vitae

Saad Murtaza Khan, PhD

Bioinformatics Specialist · Massachusetts General Hospital

Brain Tumor Research Center · Boston, MA

I am a Bioinformatics Scientist with over 10 years of experience in next-generation sequencing, including six years focused on single-cell and spatial transcriptomics in tumor biology. I currently work as a Bioinformatics Specialist at Massachusetts General Hospital, where I analyze single-cell and spatial data to define tumor cell states and how they are organized in relation to the tumor microenvironment in brain tumors. My goal is to turn computational insights into a better understanding of how tumors develop and progress, and to help guide the development of immunotherapies.

Research experience

Bioinformatics Specialist

Brain Tumor Research Center, Massachusetts General Hospital · Boston, MA

11/2022 – present
Research contributions
  • Spatial architecture of the glioblastoma immune microenvironment

    Led the analysis of a 22-sample Visium cohort with matched Xenium and single-nucleus RNA-seq, mapping tumor and immune cell states from tumor core to infiltrating edge. Used BANKSY for niche detection, RCTD and CellTrek for deconvolution, and CellChat and NicheNet for niche-restricted ligand-receptor inference. Identified interactions confined to specific anatomical niches, which argues that effective combinations will need to be chosen for the region they are meant to act on. First author on the resulting preprint.

  • Single-cell and exome characterization of meningioma

    Combined single-cell RNA-seq and whole-exome sequencing to link driver mutations to tumor and immune cell gene expression programs at single-cell resolution.

  • Epitranscriptomics of glioma

    Analyzed long-read nanopore RNA-seq to define glioma subtype-specific m6A landscapes and their effect on isoform usage and gene expression, separating the contributions of individual m6A regulators.

Bioinformatics Scientist

Brain Tumor Center, Washington University St. Louis · St. Louis, MO

12/2019 – 11/2022
Research contributions
  • Determinants of response to PD-1 blockade in brain tumors

    Analyzed single-cell transcriptomes from syngeneic glioma models to explain divergent checkpoint blockade response. GL261 tumors were enriched for PD-1-responsive progenitor exhausted CD8⁺ T cells, while CT2A tumors were dominated by PD-1-refractory terminally exhausted CD8⁺ T cells, and CD4 T cell depletion shifted the exhaustion trajectory. Co-first author in the Journal for ImmunoTherapy of Cancer.

  • Myeloid checkpoint target characterization

    Contributed the single-cell analysis supporting TREM2 as a myeloid checkpoint in glioblastoma, showing how TREM2 inhibition shifts myeloid cells toward an antitumor state (Science Advances).

  • Genetic and transcriptional evolution at AML relapse

    Integrated single-cell RNA-seq with enhanced whole-genome sequencing across paired presentation and relapse samples to link subclonal genetic change to transcriptional state. Identified relapse-enriched cell states and quiescent, stem-like subpopulations that are candidate sources of resistance.

  • Single-cell atlas of the vestibular schwannoma ecosystem

    Built a single-cell atlas of schwannoma and its microenvironment and identified a nerve injury-like program associated with myeloid infiltration, monocyte recruitment, and larger tumor size, nominating a mechanism and candidate targets (Nature Communications).

  • Reproducible pipeline infrastructure for single-cell analysis

    Designed and implemented modular WDL pipelines covering preprocessing, quality control, normalization, clustering, and differential expression, deployed on cloud and HPC through Cromwell and Terra and published as a public Dockstore collection used by the lab and outside groups. dockstore.org/organizations/PettilabMGH

Graduate Research Assistant

Informatics Institute, University of Missouri · Columbia, MO

09/2011 – 12/2019
Research contributions
  • Deep learning for RNA modification prediction

    Developed MU-PseUDeep, a convolutional neural network integrating primary sequence with predicted secondary structure to identify pseudouridine sites, outperforming existing methods across human, mouse, and yeast. First method to incorporate RNA secondary structure for this task. Genome-wide screening showed enrichment of predicted sites in neurodegeneration-associated transcripts.

  • Large-scale variant analysis for 1000 genome Soybean project

    Built a parallelized variant calling and annotation workflow on the Pegasus workflow management system, running GATK across soybean genomes on XSEDE high-performance computing at the Texas Advanced Computing Center, and delivered the results as a queryable resource in SoyKB.

  • Cancer genomics and microRNA profiling in JMML

    Analyzed whole-exome and high-depth panel sequencing from a JMML patient, identifying PI3K/AKT pathway mutations alongside known RAS/MAPK alterations, and paired this with microRNA profiling showing upregulated oncomiRs targeting PI3K/AKT genes.

  • Soybean epigenomics

    Analyzed whole-genome bisulfite and RNA-seq data to characterize inheritance of MAMP-triggered immunity in soybean, identifying heritable differentially methylated regions including variants with non-Mendelian inheritance. First genome-wide methylation survey in soybean.
